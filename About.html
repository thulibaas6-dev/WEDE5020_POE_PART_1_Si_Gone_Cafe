About Si Gone Cafe

Our Story

Located in the heart of Braamfontein, Si Gone was created to offer a vibrant, welcoming spot where great food and community come together. Whether you need your morning caffeine drive or a quiet place to study or work, Si Gone Cafe has you covered.

Our Mission

To deliver a variety of quality food and outstanding service in a comfortable environment.

Why Choose us

  • Freshly brewed coffee and teas.
  • A comfortable seating area with a calm atmosphere.
  • Affordable meals made fresh daily with healthy and indulgent choice
